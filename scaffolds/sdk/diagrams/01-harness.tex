\cruxmeta{Harness: the research loop, roles, tools and resources}{After a failed review the PI either revises and resubmits or archives the direction and restarts its context. Each phase (research, then finalize) repeats one iteration cycle: the loop prompts the PI, the PI works through its turn and briefs research assistants and Codex workers, and when the turn ends the loop either prompts again (delegate work still uncollected) or runs the phase's gate, a three-judge panel. A failing verdict becomes the next prompt; a pass moves to the next phase. A tool table and a resource table follow.}
