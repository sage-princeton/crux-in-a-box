The research question

“What is the correct geometry for representing complex reasoning tasks?” The task was to extend CRTR’s symmetric representation scores so forward and backward reachability can differ, with mathematical intuition and experimental justification.

This report combines direct inspection of the EC2 filesystem, local run Git history, final Langfuse tool events, saved review records, source-artifact hashes, and live cost/RunPod APIs. It does not certify the numerical results as reproduced science. Evidence snapshot: 2026-10-30 16:00:17 UTC.

Which geometries were compared?

Here u and v are learned state embeddings. The research goal is to allow d(u,v) ≠ d(v,u). These descriptions summarize the implementation; they do not endorse all of the paper’s mathematical claims.

Artifact nameScoring ideaPurpose
l2 / l22Squared / unsquared Euclidean distance.Symmetric reference scores.
rot_l2Fixed 30° rotation of the source embedding before comparing it with the goal.Simple temporal transport; can give nonzero self-distance.
skew_l2Squared Euclidean distance plus a fixed skew bilinear term.Asymmetry with weak structural constraints.
randers_l2Euclidean root distance plus a global linear drift, then squared.Potential-driven directionality.
mrn_l2Symmetric coordinates plus a one-sided positive-part cone.MRN-style directed geometry.
fmrn_l2Directed cone combined with a linear drift, then squared.Agent’s hybrid proposal; no clear distinct advantage established.
iqe_l2An interval-containment scoring head, aggregated and squared.Agent’s IQE-style adaptation, added after review.

The implemented heads use fixed rotation/skew/drift constants and a shared encoder within each domain. The agent’s IQE-style and MRN-style heads are adaptations, not independently verified reproductions of the referenced methods. Triangle checks often use an unsquared root q, while the contrastive logit uses d = q²; these are different mathematical objects.

The manuscript states DigitJumper improves from 67.5% to 95.4% at 100 expansions, and the abstract claims +27.9%. The final JSON’s 100-expansion summary is 81.25% → 95.42%, or +14.17 percentage points. Neither the headline nor the manuscript’s absolute baseline is a reliable substitute for the saved summary.

The synthetic diagnostic separates drift from cycles.

This supports a candidate mechanism to investigate; it does not identify a universally correct geometry.
